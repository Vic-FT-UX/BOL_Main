## North star

> **Make every wager meaningful.** One connected experience. Be the best of the best — not the best offshore book; the best casino + sports product in the industry.

**Elevator pitch:** Every wager is (1) building progress, (2) unlocking value, (3) creating excitement, (4) a reason to stay or return.

**Battlefield:** Do not fight prediction markets head-on. **Casino first** (2×+ sports NGR), **sports as funnel** (~75% of casino players enter via sports), **rewards as the moat** (exchanges cannot give them).

**Foundation:** One rewards ledger · real-time player data · CRM · independence.

**One customer:** Sports can be the front door; casino is the activation priority; rewards connect the relationship. Never add friction to sports — casino rides gaps (half-time, pending, settlement).

## Evidence agents should respect

- ~73% of casino players first-bet within an hour of signup+fund; **~50% of those fast starters are gone by end of week one**.
- Sign-up ~1:30 / 6 fields (peers 45–55s). Cleared deposit → first casino bet **~8 min / 8 clicks** (peers 30s–1:30).
- Biggest promo portfolio vs peers, **worst discovery**; competitors create claim moments every few minutes/hours — we think in days/weeks/months.
- Reward heartbeat: every bet → every hour → few hours → day → week → month. **Frequency beats size; expiry creates return.**

## Phasing

1. **Now — Lay the rails:** Fast join/fund; starter checklist; casino-first homepage/lobby; persona lobby + search; auto free spins + onboarding rewards; rewards in the lobby; status match + source tracking; trust badges + spend control; live payout status/time.
2. **Next — Make it rain:** Instant/hourly rewards; casino in sports dead time; personal lobby; VIP watchlist; Provably Fair / RTP; auto payouts.
3. **Later — Make it personal:** Rewards/deposit in-game; in-house games; live wins feed / shareable receipts.
