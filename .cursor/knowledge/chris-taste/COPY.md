# Copy — settled preference

**Source:** Chris, repeated Oct 2026 (Daily Spin tip, confirm screens, VIP blurbs).

## Never do this (AI slop)

- Marketing filler that lists every outcome: *“Win cash, free spins, or try again tomorrow.”*
- Em-dash stacks and soft qualifiers: *“Spin once a day — and every time you log in.”*
- Vague cheerleading: *“You’re in”* as a **body** headline (OK only as modal chrome title — see `CONFIRMATION_STATES.md`)
- Explaining the product back to the player in three clauses when one short fact will do

## Do this instead

- **One concrete fact.** Daily Spin tip: `One spin per login. Cash or free spins.`
- Modal subhead: `One spin per login` — not a pitch paragraph
- Prefer product nouns over hype verbs (*Added to your balance* > *Your reward has been credited to your wallet!*)
- When unsure, copy a shipped neighbor (My Bonus, RAF, VIP rakeback info) and shorten further
